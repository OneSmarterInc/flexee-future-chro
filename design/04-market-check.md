# The Future CHRO: market check of simulation publishers

24 Nov 2026. A web check of the main simulation publishers and the closest new products, done before we make any claim about the market. It uses public pages only. No demos were taken.

## Finding in one paragraph

The big publishers sell HR simulations and leadership simulations, but none of their catalogs has a simulation about AI taking over work and the expert pipeline. One small new product comes close: Future Work Academy, an 8-week AI business simulation for graduate students, with a week called "The Talent Pipeline Crisis". The 1-pager can no longer say we found no simulation like ours. It can say we found none built for executive education, with the HR leadership team as players, run by a tested numbers model, with the decisions replayed under three futures.

## What each publisher offers

| Publisher | Closest product | What it covers | AI and the pipeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harvard Business Impact (formerly Harvard Business Publishing Education) | Organizational behavior and leadership simulations: Judgment in a Crisis, Everest, Change Management: Power and Influence, Evolving Structures, Leveraging Networks | Decisions, teams, change and structure | No AI workforce or pipeline simulation in the catalog lists found. Harvard's AI work for executives is taught in workshops and research labs |
| Forio and Sim Institute | People Analytics HR simulation | HR director of a 500-agent call center over six years; policies, rewards and HR planning; 90 minutes; $168 a person | The page doesn't mention AI. Technology appears only as an event that cuts workload |
| Cesim | HR Planning Microsimulation; OnService (small service business) | HR manager in a growing organization; recruitment and cost per employee | No AI mechanics found |
| Capsim | HR module in Capstone and Foundation; CapsimInbox: Human Resources | Workforce numbers, recruiting, training; SHRM competencies in an inbox format | No AI workforce mechanics found. Capsim's own marketing now says AI made knowing cheap and judgment is still earned |
| BTS | Custom AI-first business simulations and workflow redesign workshops for client firms | Built for each client | Custom work, not a catalog product we would sell against |
| Abilitie | Director Challenge, AI Cases | Leading people and leading managers, with AI-played characters | People leadership, not workforce strategy or the pipeline |
| **Future Work Academy (Doug Mitchell)** | **The Future of Work at Apex Manufacturing** | **8 weeks in the CEO seat of a mid-market manufacturer in Iowa: automation, workforce and culture. Weekly decisions, a cast of stakeholders, AI grading against published rubrics. Graduate and undergraduate courses, plus Iowa workforce training** | **Has a week on the talent pipeline. The closest product to ours** |

## How we differ from Future Work Academy

The differences come from their public pages. A demo should confirm them before we use them in sales.

| | Future Work Academy | The Future CHRO |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Graduate and undergraduate courses; state workforce training | Executive education, EMBA, consulting firms, corporate HR teams first |
| Who plays | One person as CEO | A team as the HR leadership team, with four seats |
| Company | Manufacturer | Professional services firm, where the pipeline from analyst to partner is the business |
| What decides results | Weekly decisions graded by AI against rubrics | A tested numbers model with 22 checks. AI only voices people and stories |
| Uncertainty | Not described | Three hidden futures. Every team's decisions are replayed under all three to separate judgment from luck |
| Mechanics | Automation, workforce, culture | Workflows, review work, strain, firm knowledge, who plans AI work, the board |
