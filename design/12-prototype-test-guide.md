# The Future CHRO: prototype test guide

26 Nov 2026. For two or three testers (an executive-education colleague, an HR leader, Chuck). One sitting of about 60 minutes, alone or in a pair. Link: the Future CHRO prototype page.

## What to tell the tester

You lead the people team of Ashby Calder, an audit, tax and advisory firm of 2,000 people. You'll play three years: 2027, 2029 and 2031. Each year you read the brief, talk to the people at the table, answer one person's question, set four cards, and see two years of results. At the end you see your plan replayed in three possible futures. Think out loud. There are no right answers we're looking for.

## What to watch

1. Where they hesitate or reread. Note the screen and the words.
2. Whether they check the planning tool's work, and when.
3. Whether they find any hidden fact at the table, and which question found it.
4. What they say when the results come in, especially in 2029, when most firms that adopted AI earn less than one that didn't.
5. What they say at the replay, before and after they see their judgment.

## Questions after play

1. What did the sim teach you, in one sentence?
2. Which moment changed what you did next?
3. Which screen or words confused you?
4. Did the numbers feel believable for a firm like this? Which didn't?
5. Was 15 minutes at the table enough? Did the checking time feel like a real trade-off?
6. Would you use this in an executive program or with your own leadership team? What would need to change first?
7. What's missing that a CHRO would expect to decide?

## What we do with the answers

Each answer goes into a change list (C1, C2 and on), with Claude's recommendation and Vikram's decision, before the real build starts.
